By Air

Dublin Airport is located 10 km north of Dublin city and approximately 5 km north of DCU. The airport is serviced by many transport links from Dublin which have routes that pass by the DCU campuses.

  • Public Transport – 30 mins (Bus number 16, 41, 33): For the most up to date information on the available routes and times use the TFI Journey Planner and TFI Live App.
  • Aircoach: This is a coach service which runs from the city centre to the airport. For more information visit Aircoach.
  • Taxi: A taxi journey to DCU from Dublin airport should take 15-20 minutes and taxi pick up apps such as Free Now or Uber are available.

By Bus

The DCU Campuses’ public transport services are primarily bus-based services, with services from Dublin Bus connecting DCU with the airport and city centre. For the most up to date information on the available bus routes and times from/to DCU use the TFI Journey Planner and TFI Live App.

A map with bus stops for DCU Glasnevin campus can be found here, including stops 1644 and 1646 in Collins Avenue; stop 7571 in the Helix; stops 37 and 4680 in Ballymun Road; and stops 213 and 205 in Whitehall (Swords Road).

Bus Fares

Leap Card Fares

The TFI 90 Minute Fare (TFI 90) is a reduced rate fare that can be used for the majority of journeys in the Dublin area. It includes the option to transfer for free between Dublin City Buses, Luas, DART and commuter rail (zones 1-4 in the Short Hop Zone) within 90 mins.

TFI Leap Card Fares Adult Young Adult (19-23) / Student
Short (up to 3km) €1.50 €0.75
TFI 90 Minute €2.00 €1.00

To avail of this fare, the Leap Card is tapped on the first leg of a journey, the commuter can then change transport mode as often as needed and then touch on again at the start of the last leg of the journey. Once the commuter touches on the last journey within 90 minutes of the first they will only be charged the TFI 90 Fare.

You can buy your TFI Leap Card:
  • At any available Leap card agent outlets - find your nearest agent using the map under the Where to Buy section in the TFI Leap Card website.
  • Online in the Buy section of the TFI Leap Card website.
  • You can also buy at selected DART ticket machines in Dublin.

A deposit of €5 with a minimum top up of €5 is required when purchasing.

Cash Fares

Cash fares on Dublin Bus are higher than Leap Card fares and require exact change in coins, as drivers cannot provide change or accept banknotes.
