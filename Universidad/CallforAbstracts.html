Call for Abstracts

Promoting innovation and integration of saliva science in higher education

We are pleased to invite educators, researchers, and professionals to submit abstracts for this event, which aims to promote innovation and the integration of saliva science in higher education, while providing a platform for sharing knowledge across research, education, and clinical practice.

Scope of the Event

We welcome contributions related to saliva science in a wide range of areas, including:

  • Basic and applied research
  • Clinical applications and diagnostics
  • Biomarker discovery and validation
  • Educational innovation and teaching methodologies
  • Interdisciplinary approaches involving saliva analysis

Presentation Formats

Accepted abstracts will be presented as:

  • Oral presentations
  • Poster presentations
Online submission portal opens June 1st, 2026
Deadline for abstracts submission June 25th, 2026 (Extended)
Presenting author registration deadline July 30th, 2026

Submission Guidelines

Please review the following guidelines carefully before submitting your abstract:

  1. The presenting author must complete registration for the event by 30th July 2026.
  2. Participants presenting a work, in oral or poster format, will have to attend in-person.
  3. It is the responsibility of the presenting author to ensure that all co-authors have reviewed the abstract and approved its submission.
  4. Each participant may submit a maximum of five abstracts, irrespective of authorship role, being able to act as presenting author for up to three abstracts.
  5. Submissions must be completed online through the official website (link will be available from June 1st, 2026). Abstracts sent by email or other means will not be considered. Abstract template is at the end of this page.
  6. Only original work will be accepted. Abstracts must not have been previously published or presented at another scientific meeting.
  7. Authors are required to declare any potential conflicts of interest during the submission process.
  8. All studies must adhere to relevant ethical standards, including animal welfare regulations and data protection requirements.

Check list for abstracts

  • Abstracts should be written in clear and concise English, using Times New Roman 12, single space.
  • Abstract length should not exceed 200 words (excluding title, authors, affiliations, and keywords).
  • Abstracts must contain a TITLE, author names and affiliations.
  • The presenting author should be identified by underlining his/her last name.
  • Maximum of 5 keywords.
  • Word or pdf file.
  • Preferred Presentation type: oral or poster.
